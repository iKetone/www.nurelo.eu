A look behind the alarm

What happens after the button is pressed.

Follow an alarm from the wireless call button to billing and see where Nurelo takes part.

  1. At the participant's home

    One press, one documented call-out.

    The alarm is matched to the participant and their device. When someone attends, the call-out is documented.

  2. In the call centre

    The alarm arrives where it is needed.

    Nurelo forwards the alarm to the emergency call centre. False and test alarms are recorded and evaluated.

  3. Administration

    The monthly fee bills itself.

    Manage participants, devices and contracts in one place. The monthly flat fee is billed automatically.

  4. From alarm to billing

    Every step traceable.

    From the alarm through the call-out to billing, every step stays documented, including false and test alarms.
