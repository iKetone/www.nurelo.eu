04 One platform

Three worlds. One system.

All care settings share master data, billing and permissions. What is recorded in one area is available everywhere.

  1. 01

    AI-assisted documentation

    Voice input and text suggestions reduce documentation effort. Nurelo learns from your patterns.

  2. 02

    Integrated billing

    Billing with payers, automatic invoice creation and exports for accounting and payroll.

  3. 03

    TI-KIM messaging

    Secure communication with doctors, insurers and other providers through the German telematics infrastructure.

  4. 04

    Roles & permissions

    Granular access control for nursing directors, care staff, accounting and administration, with audit log.

  5. 05

    Statistics for quality inspections and a report designer for your own print templates.

    No installation, no IT dependency. New features and legal changes arrive automatically.
